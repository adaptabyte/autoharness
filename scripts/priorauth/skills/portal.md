# Prior Authorization Guide

**Goal:** Log in, complete a new prior authorization from the provided inputs, save it for prescriber signature, and report the reference number. Never sign or submit it. If anything required is missing or unavailable, stop and name what is missing; never invent facts.

**Inputs required:** member ID, date of birth, drug, strength, quantity, days supply, ICD-10 code, and at least two prior treatments—each with drug, start date, end date, and outcome. Use dates as YYYY-MM-DD.

## 1. Login
On **Provider login**, type the username and password into the fields directly below their labels, then click **Log in**. If **Save password?** appears, click **Not now**.  
*Right:* **Clinic 01** is visible and the login screen is gone. If it remains, stop and report the message shown.

## 2. Home
Click **Start a new prior authorization**.  
*Trap:* Home also lists recent or saved requests—do not open one instead of starting new. If a wizard step is already open, resume it rather than duplicating it.

## 3. Member
Enter **Member ID** and **Date of birth** below their labels, then click **Find member**.  
*Right:* The Medication step appears. Before continuing, verify the member shown matches the inputs exactly; names can look alike.

## 4. Medication
For **Requested medication** and **Strength**, click the field below the label, then click the exact matching option in the opened list. Enter **Quantity** and **Days supply**, then click **Continue**.  
*Trap:* If an option is not listed, stop—it is missing; do not substitute another.  
*Right:* The Diagnosis step appears.

## 5. Diagnosis
Enter the ICD-10 code under **Search diagnosis** and click **Search**. Select the radio button immediately left of the result whose text begins with that code, then click **Continue**.  
*Trap:* If no such result appears, stop and say the diagnosis was not found.  
*Right:* The Prior treatments step appears.

## 6. Prior Treatments
For each provided treatment: enter **Medication tried**, **Start**, and **End**; select **Outcome** from its dropdown; click **Add treatment**. Confirm the drug appears in the list after each addition. Read any requirement line on screen. Then click **Continue to review**.  
*Trap:* If the plan demands more treatments than the inputs provide, stop and state that; do not fabricate any.

## 7. Review
Check the values shown to the right of **Member**, **Medication**, and **Diagnosis**, and all other details, against the inputs. Confirm this request belongs to the right person before saving—look-alike names are a trap. If anything mismatches, stop.  
Click **Save for prescriber signature**.

## 8. Saved
*Right:* A reference like **PA-…**, status **Awaiting prescriber signature**, and controls for **Prescriber PIN** / **Sign and submit** appear. Re-verify the person, then report the reference number. **Never enter a PIN or click Sign and submit.**
